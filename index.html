Sarah Chen

Senior Product Designer & Systems Strategist

About Me

I am an end-to-end product designer based in San Francisco with over eight years of experience translating complex technical problems into intuitive, human-centered digital experiences. My work sits at the intersection of UX architecture, design systems, and emerging consumer technology. I thrive in cross-functional environments where I can bridge the gap between engineering constraints and user needs, leading products from early concept discovery to large-scale market launch.

Experience

Over the past decade, I have driven product strategy across both high-growth startups and established enterprise teams. Most recently as Senior Product Designer at Apex Tech, I led the redesign of our core web platform, improving user onboarding retention by 35% and establishing a global design system used across five distinct product lines. Prior to that, I spent three years at Studio North managing digital product design for fintech and healthcare clients, facilitating user research sprints, and mentoring junior designers.

Fields of Intrest

My professional focus is centered around accessibility (WCAG compliance), design system architecture, and spatial computing interfaces. I am particularly fascinated by how AI-driven personalization can reshape user interfaces without sacrificing user agency or privacy. Outside of work, I explore generative art, urban micro-photography, and sustainable interaction design for IoT home devices.

Projects

One of my primary projects is FlowKit, an open-source design system tool that helps solo developers auto-generate accessible color palettes and typographic hierarchies. I also spearheaded Pulse, a mobile wellness dashboard designed specifically for shift workers, which earned an IxDA Design Award nomination in 2024. My personal design lab consistently publishes articles on modern component architecture and user research methodologies.

Education

I hold a Bachelor of Science in Human-Computer Interaction with a minor in Computer Science from the University of California, Berkeley (2014–2018). My coursework focused on user research methodologies, cognitive psychology, front-end web development, and interaction design, laying a technical and analytical foundation for my design career.
